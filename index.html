01 / 02
Digital Stress Meter
A hardware-software integrated system that measures and displays physiological stress levels in real time. The device captures bio-signals such as heart rate and skin conductance via sensors, processes the data using a microcontroller programmed in C, and outputs a numerical stress index on a digital display. Designed to assist individuals in monitoring mental and physical stress for health awareness and early intervention.
C Language Microcontroller Bio-Sensors Embedded Systems

Emergency Response Drone System
An autonomous drone system engineered for rapid emergency response in disaster or accident scenarios. The drone is equipped with GPS navigation, real-time video transmission, and a payload delivery mechanism for first-aid supplies. Programmed with automated flight path logic, it can be remotely dispatched to coordinates and relay live footage to a command interface — reducing human risk in high-danger situations and cutting response time significantly.
